# Product shots

The `Qué es` line selects the shot. For product work the brief is identical to any other image: attach the reference photo and fill the same seven lines.

## Promotional image

Describe the composition, not the product. Light, product position and its relationship to the title stay open unless you specify them.

> `Tema`: the microphone in the foreground, large, filling the lower half, with an oversized title behind it cut by the mic.

## Shot vocabulary

| Shot | Purpose | How to ask | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exploded view | parts and assembly | parts separated and floating on their axis | parts exist, none duplicated |
| X-ray | see the interior | semi-transparent shell with components indicated | interior is documented |
| Cutaway | internal layers | object cut by a plane | layout and materials are real |
| Blueprint | technical views | thin lines, front and side view | geometry, no invented measurements |
| Infographic | explain functions | product with exact text blocks | data, labels, legibility |
| Isometric | schematic volume | isometric perspective illustration | proportions and orientation |
| Knolling | arrange a kit | top view, objects parallel and at right angles | full inventory, no extras |
| Labeled diagram | point at parts | leader lines to specific labels | every label points at its part |
| Product anatomy | teardown plus explanation | editorial sheet with parts and labels | correspondence with the real product |
| Packaging | explore the container | box mockup or unfolded dieline | text, measurements, feasibility |

Short visual terms work on their own. Attach the photo and write `knolling` or `blueprint`. When you need tighter control, add the limits:

> Create a top-down knolling photograph with the objects from the attached list. Arrange them parallel and at right angles on a neutral background. No extra parts, no text.

> Create a blueprint sheet of the product in the photo, white lines on blue, front and side views. Preserve the visible geometry. No dimensions, no numeric callouts.

## Fidelity rules

- The reference photo always accompanies the prompt.
- Asking for fidelity directs the generation; it does not replace verification.
- Check proportions, base, grille and logo against the reference before presenting.
- A concept render is not documentation. If the interior is invented, say so when publishing.
- An exterior photo carries no interior information. An exploded view needs a parts list or documentation.
